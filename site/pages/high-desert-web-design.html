What local businesses usually need

  • A site that works on a phone. Most people look up a plumber, barber, food truck or contractor on their phone. Every build is designed phone-first, then checked on tablet and desktop.
  • A Google Business Profile that is set up right. The right categories, services, hours and photos help people find you on Google Search and Maps. We do not promise rankings, because nobody can.
  • A clear way to contact or book you. A call button, a simple form with spam protection, or a booking link, whichever fits how you take work.
  • Basic security. HTTPS, a security-header review and fewer third-party scripts. No site is unhackable, and we tell you plainly what risk remains.
