Frequently asked questions

How much does a small business website cost?

A starter website with up to 5 pages is $1,500. Larger builds are $3,500 or $7,500 depending on pages and features. All package prices are one-time. Care plans are optional and billed monthly.

How long does a website take?

About 2 to 3 weeks for a starter site, 4 to 5 weeks for the Samurai Site and 6 to 8 weeks for the Shogun Experience. A Google Business Profile tune-up takes about a week.

What is a Google Business Profile tune-up?

An audit and rewrite of your Google listing: the right categories, services and hours, a photo plan and reply templates for reviews. It helps people find you in local search and on Maps. It does not guarantee a ranking.

Will my website be secure?

Every build includes HTTPS and a security-header review, spam protection on forms, and a short list of third-party scripts. No website can be made completely unhackable, so we reduce risk and tell you plainly what remains.

Do I need a monthly care plan?

No. Care plans are optional. They cover hosting, monitoring and small edits so you do not have to.

Do you offer web3 services?

Read-only web3 integrations are available by quote only. We do not take custody of funds, keys or seed phrases, and we will never ask for your seed phrase or private key.

How do I pay?

Start with a free 15-minute call. Once we agree on the scope, we send a written proposal that spells out the price, billing, and refund or cancellation terms before you pay anything. Nothing is charged through this website.

Who owns my website?

You own your domain and your content. The hand-off terms for the finished site are written into your proposal before you pay, so there are no surprises. A care plan is optional and covers hosting, monitoring and edits.

What if my project is not the right fit?

We will tell you on the free call. If what you need is outside what we do well, we will say so plainly and point you toward a better option where we can.

Can I see your work?

Yes. Our portfolio is this site and clearly labeled concept projects. See the Work page. We show real client work only with the client's written permission.
